# Fonts

Latin subsets served by Google Fonts, copied here so the page works offline
(no CDN — DECISIONS 2026-08-24 "Page redesign"). Both are licensed under the

- Space Grotesk 400/500/700 — Florian Karsten — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk
- IBM Plex Mono 400/500 — IBM — https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono

Two families, not three: the studio-console system sets display *and* body
in Space Grotesk and keeps the mono for figures only (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md,
"Type"). Barlow Condensed, Source Serif 4 and JetBrains Mono were removed
with the practice-desk palette they belonged to.
