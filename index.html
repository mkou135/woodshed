Woodshed

Drop a transcribed solo. Woodshed finds the ideas in it and turns each one into four things to practise.

Drop a transcription here .mxl or .musicxml from MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale

Then, for every idea it finds

  1. 1Loop it

    As played, with the record.

  2. 2Through the tune

    Every chord that takes it.

  3. 3Vary it

    Moved, reversed, recombined.

  4. 4Write your own

    Then check it here.
